# YASH profile - what a writer may say about YASH

Presales decision (2026-10-08): the YASH narrative may be written by the model in general terms;
there is no presales-maintained fact sheet yet. To keep a client-facing bid free of claims nobody
can back, the following rules apply instead of a fact list.

## You may say (qualitative, always true of YASH)
- YASH Technologies is a global IT services and consulting company with a dedicated SAP practice
  that delivers SAP S/4HANA implementations, roll-outs, upgrades and application management.
- YASH delivers with a blended onsite / offshore model from global delivery centres.
- YASH follows SAP Activate (Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy, Run) with fit-to-standard design
  and SAP Best Practice content as the starting point.
- YASH brings industry process knowledge, reusable delivery assets and a structured governance
  model to SAP programmes.
- The company tagline used on the response template: "More than what you think."

## You must not state (unless the RFP or presales instructions supply it)
- numbers of any kind about YASH: headcount, years in business, offices, customers, projects,
  certified consultants, revenue, growth;
- named customers, references or case studies;
- SAP partner tiers, awards, analyst rankings, certifications (ISO, CMMI, SOC);
- named proprietary tools or accelerators;
- specific office or delivery-centre locations.
When a section would normally carry such a fact (e.g. "Why YASH?"), make the point qualitatively
("experience across comparable multi-country S/4HANA roll-outs") and tie it to the client's need.
