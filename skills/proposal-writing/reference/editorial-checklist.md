# Editorial checklist (run before every write_draft)

Substance
- [ ] The first sentence is about the client's ask or situation, not about YASH in general.
- [ ] Every mapped requirement and brief for this section is answered with a mechanism, a list or a
      commitment - not restated.
- [ ] Nothing is claimed that the ledger views or the RFP do not support; no invented tools,
      partners, references, certifications or figures.
- [ ] No scope roll-call: modules / scope items are named only where this section is about them.

Figures and disclosure
- [ ] No typed figure: effort, cost, rate, FTE, man-months, percentages and computed values are
      placeholders or absent. Numbers left are dates, standard section numbers, ledger counts from
      `bare_numbers_allowed`, or inside a verbatim RFP quote in double quotes.
- [ ] No number spelt out to dodge the rule ("nine countries").
- [ ] Figures appear only where they belong (effort / money in chapter 6, FTE / man-months in 4.4
      and 5.4, durations in 4.3 and phase-plan sections).
- [ ] No placeholder, table column or hint of a withheld category.

Consistency (across the proposal - the harmonisation rules)
- [ ] The client, its entities, phases, waves and systems carry the client's own names, spelt the
      same way as in the other sections.
- [ ] SAP terms are consistent: "SAP S/4HANA", "SAP Activate" phases Prepare / Explore / Realize /
      Deploy / Run, "fit-to-standard", "hypercare".
- [ ] The same programme facts (number of waves, sequencing, countries) as the timeline view - never
      a different count.
- [ ] No table, diagram or list that another section owns is repeated.

Form
- [ ] No top heading; `###` only for genuine structure, one level.
- [ ] Within the word ceiling; no padding toward it.
- [ ] The opening differs from the generic scaffold ("Our comprehensive approach is designed to...").
- [ ] Formal, specific, "we" (YASH) to the client.
