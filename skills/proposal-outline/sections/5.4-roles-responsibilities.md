# 5.4 Roles and Responsibilities (intro before `{{table:role_roster}}`)

Introduce how roles and responsibilities are organised in 3-4 sentences before the role roster
that follows. Cover the onsite/offshore split, who owns which workstream, and how the client's own
team is expected to engage. Do not list the roles or their man-months yourself; the table does
that.

Disclosure conditions:
- The renderer appends the roster only when `resource_allocation` is not withheld: Role | Location
  | one column per wave | Total Man-months. It drops the Location column when `resource_location`
  is withheld.
- Mention the onsite/offshore split only when `resource_location` is not withheld.
- When `resource_allocation` is withheld, there is no roster, so your prose carries the
  responsibilities. Describe each role from `team` and what it owns. A qualitative pipe table
  (Role | Key responsibilities) is fine. Add no FTE, man-month or location figures. In this case
  the ceiling stays the same; use it.
