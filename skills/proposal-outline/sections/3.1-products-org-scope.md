# 3.1 Products and Organizational Scope (intro before `{{table:module_summary}}`)

Introduce the products and organisational scope in 3-4 sentences before the scope summary table
that follows:
- which SAP solution is being implemented;
- for which business areas;
- across which countries and legal entities, per the client's own RFP.

Do not state scope-item counts or effort figures yourself; the table does that.

The renderer appends the table after your text: Module | Business Area | SAP Scope Items | Effort
(person-days), with module rows, business-area sub-rows and a total row. It drops the count column
when `scope_item_detail` is withheld and the effort column when `effort_detail` is withheld. Do
not repeat the table, and do not promise a column the disclosure profile removes.
