# 6.2 Cost Estimates (intro before `{{table:cost}}`)

Briefly introduce the cost estimate before the table. Do not state numbers yourself.

The renderer appends the commercial table (Item | Value). It holds the total project effort and
the hypercare effort when effort is disclosed, the implementation effort excluding hypercare, the
blended implementation rate (USD/day), the hypercare rate, and the total project cost. The total
is computed from the rows shown.
- Say what the price covers: implementation at a blended day rate, hypercare at its own rate, and
  inclusion of the non-catalogue tools and integrations. Say that the figures are taken from the
  reviewed estimate.
- When `commercial_detail` is withheld, the renderer omits the table. Describe the commercial model
  only (time-and-materials or fixed price as the RFP or ledger states, blended rate basis,
  hypercare priced separately), not its figures.
- When a client requirement mapped here explicitly asks for a value in the text, use
  `{{fig:total_project_cost}}`, `{{fig:daily_rate}}` or `{{fig:hypercare_rate}}`, and only when
  commercial detail is disclosed.
