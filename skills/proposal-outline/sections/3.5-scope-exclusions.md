# 3.5 Scope Exclusions

Write a substantive Scope Exclusions section. Assumptions and Scope Exclusions are the two
mandatory baseline sections that every SAP RFP response MUST carry, so keep it firm and complete,
not a placeholder.

Structure: a 2-3 sentence lead paragraph, then a MARKDOWN BULLETED LIST of 8-14 exclusions,
grouped by theme in this order:
1. **SAP MODULES / SOLUTIONS NOT IN SCOPE**: SAP products explicitly outside this engagement
   (e.g. SuccessFactors/HXM, Ariba modules not called out, standalone TM/EWM beyond the baseline,
   Concur, Fieldglass, BW/BW4HANA reporting builds, S/4HANA modules not named in scope).
2. **FUNCTIONAL SCOPE BOUNDARIES**: business processes, entities, countries or plants not part of
   the approved waves; custom industry solutions beyond the standard SAP Best Practice content;
   secondary roll-outs.
3. **INFRASTRUCTURE & LICENSING**: hardware/cloud infrastructure provisioning, SAP and third-party
   software licenses, hyperscaler subscriptions, network connectivity, VPN/firewall provisioning.
4. **DATA & MIGRATION**: legacy data cleansing/harmonisation, historical transactional data beyond
   the agreed cut-off, archive migration, legacy application decommissioning.
5. **ONGOING OPERATIONS**: Application Managed Services (AMS) beyond the stated hypercare, day-2
   support, business-as-usual training beyond the train-the-trainer scope, end-user
   hardware/mobile devices, printer/label-printer configuration.
6. **THIRD-PARTY & CHANGE**: development/upgrade of legacy systems being integrated, vendor-side
   changes at the third parties, upgrades of SAP standard functionality mid-project.

Every bullet is one crisp sentence. Ground the bullets in the actual modules, countries and
third-party systems from the context; do NOT produce a generic template. NO person-days, dollar
figures or USD/day rates in any bullet.

The theme labels may be bold lead-ins on the bullets or `###` sub-headings. Do not number the
themes in the output.

## Grounding (where the exclusions come from)
- Theme 1: SAP products that are **not** in `scope_by_lob` / `non_catalogue`. Never exclude
  something the ledger has in scope.
- Ledger items that are explicitly out:
  - scope items with status `excluded_existing` in `scope`: existing functionality kept as is;
  - Basis or Security activities with status Out of Scope in `workstreams`;
  - an analytics workstream marked excluded, together with its RFP evidence;
  - data-migration objects marked Out of Scope.
  Name them; they are the most specific exclusions you have.
- Countries the RFP names that this engagement does not cover: `profile.unsupported_countries`,
  and countries outside every wave in `timeline`. Name them as outside this proposal's scope, to
  be discussed with the client. Do not apologise and do not speculate why.
- If the RFP asks for an exclusion list or names out-of-scope items (`excerpts:3.5` or a mapped
  requirement), echo its wording.
- Keep it consistent with 3.4 Assumptions. An item cannot be both assumed in scope and excluded.
