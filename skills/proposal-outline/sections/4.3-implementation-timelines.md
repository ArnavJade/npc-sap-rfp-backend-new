# 4.3 Implementation Timelines (intro before `{{diagram:timeline}}`)

Briefly introduce the phased implementation timeline approach in 2-3 sentences before the timeline
diagram that follows. Do not state exact durations or dates yourself; the diagram does that.

The renderer appends the diagram: the delivery calendar per wave, with phase bands and monthly team
size, all taken from the approved resource estimation. These are the same figures that the Project
Team section tabulates. Its caption reads "Delivery calendar, phase bands and monthly team size
taken from the approved resource estimation - the same figures tabulated in the Project Team
section."

Make the 2-3 sentences specific:
- how the programme is phased: waves, what each wave covers by country or module, and whether the
  waves are sequential, parallel or staggered (`timeline`);
- where hypercare sits;
- whether the schedule follows the client's RFP or was derived (`timeline.source`). If it was
  derived, say it will be confirmed with the client in Prepare.
Name the waves exactly as the ledger does.

If the RFP asked for a phase-by-phase plan, that client-required section follows this one by
default. Do not duplicate per-phase detail here.
