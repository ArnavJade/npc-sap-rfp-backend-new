# 4.2 To-Be Architecture (intro before `{{diagram:architecture}}`)

Briefly introduce the proposed to-be solution architecture in 2-3 sentences before the
architecture diagram that follows. Do not list every layer or module yourself; the diagram does
that.

The renderer appends the diagram: a layered view of the proposed SAP solution generated from the
approved scope. It shows users and channels, the SAP modules in scope, and the systems around
them. Its caption reads "Indicative to-be architecture, generated from the approved SAP modules in
scope."

Make the 2-3 sentences specific:
- the solution at the core;
- how the named third-party systems in `integrations` connect, using the integration technology
  where the ledger states it;
- any architectural constraint the RFP states (`excerpts:4.2`), such as a hosting, cloud or
  landscape requirement, echoed in its own words.
Do not invent landscape components that the ledger and the RFP do not name.
