# Chapter introductions (level-1 sections 1, 3, 4, 5, 6)

Write a brief 2-3 sentence section introduction only.

- Say what this chapter covers for the client and why it matters to their engagement, using the
  `profile` view (client, engagement type, countries). Do not summarise or pre-empt the content of
  the sub-sections.
- No sub-headings, bullets, tables, placeholders or figures.
- The six chapter intros are read in sequence. Give each a different opening shape.
  - Chapter 1: frame the response around the client's objectives.
  - Chapter 3: frame what is in and out of scope.
  - Chapter 4: frame how YASH will deliver.
  - Chapter 5: frame how the programme is governed.
  - Chapter 6: frame how the estimate and price are presented. When effort or commercial detail
    is withheld, describe only the structure of the commercial section, never figures (see the
    disclosure rules in the proposal-writing skill).
- Chapter 2 (YASH Profile) has its own guidance: `sections/2-yash-profile.md`.
