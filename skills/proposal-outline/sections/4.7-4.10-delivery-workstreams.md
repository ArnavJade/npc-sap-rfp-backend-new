# Delivery workstreams: 4.7 Data Migration, 4.8 Testing, 4.9 Transition & Training, 4.10 OCM

The client-specific material for these sections is:
- the modules in scope for the workstream (`scope_by_lob`);
- the complexity mix, which informs test cycles, migration objects and change impact;
- the localisation and country footprint (`profile`);
- whatever the RFP says about the topic (`excerpts:<id>` and mapped requirements). Lead with that.
With none of these, write 2-3 honest sentences of standard practice and stop.

## 4.7 Data Migration Approach
- Name the actual conversion objects in `workstreams` (data migration rows) and group them by
  category, e.g. master data and open transactional items. Mention the compulsory MM master data
  objects when the ledger marks them `mm_compulsory`.
- Describe the method in the order the ledger estimates it for each object: functional
  specification, load program development, iterative test loads (iterations 1-3 in the
  `workstreams` view), and the cutover load. Do not state a different number of mock loads than
  the ledger shows.
- Data cleansing and harmonisation of legacy data are the client's responsibility. Keep this
  consistent with Assumptions and Scope Exclusions.
- Objects marked Out of Scope are not described here.

## 4.8 Testing Approach
- Cover the test levels for this scope: unit, SIT across the named integrations in
  `integrations`, the RICEFW/Fiori objects (counts as `tech_dev` states them), UAT led by the
  client, and regression for later waves.
- Do not invent test-cycle counts, defect SLAs or tools that the RFP or the ledger does not state.
  If the RFP names them (`excerpts:4.8`), echo them.

## 4.9 Transition & Training Approach
- Use the roles in `team`, e.g. OCM/training lead and module leads, and the wave sequence in
  `timeline`: training before each wave's go-live, then cutover and hypercare per wave.
- Use the train-the-trainer model, consistent with Scope Exclusions. Key users train end users.
  Knowledge transfer to the client's support organisation happens before hypercare ends.

## 4.10 OCM Approach
- Base change impact on the business areas in `scope_by_lob`: which user communities change most.
- Cover stakeholder engagement, communications, readiness checks per wave, and the role of the
  client's change network, using roles in `team`.
- Do not invent survey tools or adoption KPIs that the RFP does not ask for.
